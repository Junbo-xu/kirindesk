# Privacy Policy

**Status: DRAFT — Phase 0**

This document is a framework draft. It has not been reviewed by legal counsel and must not be published externally without legal review.

## Purpose

Define how KirinDesk collects, uses, stores, and protects personal and business data.

## 1. Data We Collect

- Account information (name, email, organization)
- Business data created by customers within the platform
- Usage logs and platform operational metrics
- Payment information (processed by third-party payment providers)

## 2. How We Use Data

- To provide and maintain the service
- To communicate with users about their account
- To improve platform performance and reliability
- NOT for resale, resource copying, or public AI training

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

- Customer business data is not shared with third parties
- Infrastructure subprocessors are listed in subprocessors.md
- Payment processing delegated to certified payment providers
- No data sold to advertisers or data brokers

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Data stored on infrastructure in the region disclosed to customers
- Access controls enforced at application and database levels
- Encryption in transit (TLS) for all communications
- Encryption at rest planned for future phases

## 5. User Rights

- Access: Request a copy of your data
- Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
- Deletion: Request deletion of your data
- Export: Request data export in standard formats
- Objection: Object to specific processing activities

## 6. Cookies and Tracking

- Essential cookies for authentication and session management
- No third-party advertising trackers
- Analytics (if implemented) will be disclosed

## 7. Policy Changes

- Users will be notified of material changes
- Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance

## Phase 0 Constraints

This is a draft framework. User rights mechanisms (export, deletion) are not yet implemented. Technical privacy controls begin in Phase 1+.

## Next Implementation Phases

- Phase 1: Basic access controls, audit logging
- Phase 2: Data export tooling, deletion workflow
- Phase 3: Consent management, cookie controls
- Future: Regional data residency options

## Prohibited Claims

This policy does not claim GDPR compliance, PDPA compliance, or any regulatory certification until independently verified.
